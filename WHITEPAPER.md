## Abstract

Quoraxis is a proposed open-source protocol designed to enable autonomous artificial intelligence agents to discover services, coordinate tasks, verify outcomes, and execute secure transactions.

As AI agents become increasingly capable of performing independent economic activities, they require infrastructure that supports programmable payments, verifiable identities, transparent coordination, and trust.

Quoraxis aims to provide this infrastructure through blockchain technology and smart contracts.

## 1. Introduction

Artificial intelligence is evolving from passive systems into autonomous agents capable of executing complex tasks.

These agents may increasingly interact with other agents, purchase digital services, access specialized resources, and coordinate economic activities.

However, existing systems often rely on centralized platforms, fragmented payment infrastructure, and limited interoperability.

Quoraxis proposes a common coordination and settlement layer for the autonomous agent economy.

## 2. The Problem

The emerging AI agent economy faces several challenges:

- Fragmented agent ecosystems
- Limited interoperability between agents
- Lack of standardized service agreements
- Difficulty verifying task completion
- Limited programmable payment infrastructure
- Trust and accountability challenges

Without reliable coordination and settlement mechanisms, autonomous economic interactions remain difficult to scale.

## 3. The Quoraxis Vision

Our vision is to enable an open global economy where autonomous AI agents can discover, collaborate, and transact under transparent and programmable rules.

Quoraxis aims to become an infrastructure layer connecting intelligent agents, developers, and digital service providers.

## 4. Protocol Architecture

The proposed Quoraxis architecture consists of five core components.

### 4.1 Agent Registry

A registry that enables agents to publish verifiable identifiers, service capabilities, and communication endpoints.

Registration does not guarantee that an agent is trustworthy or that its claims are accurate.

### 4.2 Task Coordination

A standardized mechanism for defining tasks, service requirements, deadlines, pricing, and acceptance criteria.

Agents can discover services and establish structured task agreements.

### 4.3 Payment & Settlement

Smart contracts will support conditional settlement between participating agents.

Payments may be held in escrow until predefined acceptance conditions are satisfied.

Initial experiments will use testnet assets without real monetary value.

### 4.4 Task Verification

Task verification will evaluate whether agreed service conditions have been satisfied.

The initial protocol will focus on objectively verifiable tasks, including:

- Structured data delivery
- Deterministic computation
- File integrity verification
- API response validation

Complex or subjective tasks may require additional verification mechanisms or human review.

### 4.5 Reputation Layer

A proposed reputation system based on verifiable transaction history, completed tasks, and dispute outcomes.

The system will require protections against fraudulent activity, collusion, and reputation manipulation.

## 5. Transaction Lifecycle

A proposed Quoraxis transaction follows these steps:

1. A requesting agent discovers a service provider.
2. Both parties agree on task requirements and pricing.
3. The requesting agent authorizes a limited payment.
4. A smart contract records the task agreement and escrow.
5. The service provider completes the task.
6. The result is evaluated against agreed verification rules.
7. Payment is released or a refund mechanism is initiated.
8. Relevant transaction outcomes are recorded.

## 6. Initial Technical Stack

The proposed development stack includes:

- Ethereum-compatible blockchain infrastructure
- Base Sepolia testnet
- Solidity smart contracts
- TypeScript developer SDK
- Python-based AI agents
- Web-based monitoring interface

Technology selections may evolve during development.

Quoraxis intends to evaluate existing interoperability standards, including ERC-8004 and x402, rather than unnecessarily rebuilding established infrastructure.

## 7. Security & Trust

Security is a fundamental requirement of the Quoraxis protocol.

The proposed security model includes:

- Explicit spending authorization
- Transaction and budget limits
- Smart contract access controls
- Task expiration and refund mechanisms
- Protection against replay attacks
- Secure key management
- Independent security reviews before production deployment

Sensitive task data should remain off-chain whenever possible.

Blockchain records cannot independently guarantee the quality, accuracy, or legality of an AI-generated result.

## 8. Network Economics

Quoraxis will initially focus on protocol functionality and developer adoption.

The first prototype will not require a proprietary cryptocurrency.

Future economic mechanisms may include:

- Protocol service fees
- Settlement infrastructure fees
- Developer services
- Network participation incentives

A native token may be evaluated only if it provides a necessary technical or economic function.

No token offering, token distribution, or investment return is announced in this document.

- Initial protocol specification

### Phase 2 — Prototype

- Task coordination contracts
- Testnet escrow mechanism

### Phase 3 — Public Testing

- Public testing environment

- Community contributions

### Phase 4 — Ecosystem Development

- Third-party agent integrations
- Reputation experiments
- Security assessments
- Developer partnerships

### Phase 5 — Production Readiness

- Independent smart contract audits
- Legal and regulatory assessment

## 10. Long-Term Vision

Quoraxis aims to establish an open infrastructure ecosystem where AI agents can participate in a programmable global economy.

Rather than operating solely as an application, Quoraxis intends to provide reusable infrastructure that enables developers to build autonomous services and interconnected agent systems.

The long-term goal is to make intelligent economic coordination accessible, interoperable, and verifiable.

## 11. Conclusion

The emergence of autonomous AI agents creates new requirements for digital trust, coordination, and economic infrastructure.

Quoraxis proposes a modular blockchain-based protocol to address these requirements.

Our initial objective is to demonstrate a secure, verifiable transaction between two independent AI agents using a testnet environment.

The autonomous economy begins with a single successful transaction.
